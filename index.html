Frequently Asked Questions

What is TDEE?
Total Daily Energy Expenditure — the total calories you burn per day including exercise and daily activity.
What formula is used?
The Mifflin-St Jeor equation, widely considered the most accurate BMR formula.
Is this free?
Yes — completely free, no sign-up needed.
How accurate is it?
It's an estimate — individual metabolism varies. Use it as a starting point and consult a nutritionist for personalised advice.
